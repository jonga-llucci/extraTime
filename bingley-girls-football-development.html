The current programme

Additional training. Individual development.

Extra Time Coaching runs a girls’ football development centre at Beckfoot School in Bingley. The sessions are for U12–U15 players with grassroots football experience who want extra opportunities to practise, improve technique and build confidence.

Sessions are shaped around the players attending. Development themes can include passing and receiving, scanning and movement, ball mastery, 1v1s, striking, defending, shielding and decision making. Small groups allow players to repeat actions, receive individual feedback and work on relevant areas of their game.

Extra Time Coaching is independent and is not a competitive team or a replacement for club football. Players remain part of their own grassroots teams and can attend regardless of which club they play for.

Who is it for?

U12–U15 girls

For players already involved in grassroots football. These are not beginners’ sessions.

Any grassroots club

The centre is independent. Players from different clubs are welcome, and there is no recruitment into a team.

Individual development

The focus is on technical actions, confidence and player-specific needs rather than preparing a team for fixtures.
