About the U10–U12 centre

Extra Time Coaching is planning a girls football development centre for ages 10–12 at Beckfoot School in Bingley, alongside our U13–U15 centre. It is a small-group centre for girls who want extra opportunities to practise, improve technique and build confidence in addition to their grassroots club football.

Extra Time Coaching is independent and is not a competitive team or a replacement for club football. Players remain part of their own grassroots teams and can attend regardless of which club they play for.

Not open for booking yet. Days and times are still to be confirmed. To add your player to the waiting list, use the enquiry form and choose “Girls U10–U12 Waiting List”.

Girls’ football coaching for ages 10–12 near Bingley

The Beckfoot School location is suitable for families looking for girls football training for ages 10 to 12 in Bingley and nearby areas, including Eldwick, Saltaire, Keighley and Bradford.

Safe, qualified and accredited

Extra Time Coaching is a 1 star England Football accredited club. For youth football, that means DBS checked coaches and officials and a trained club welfare officer, and all players are insured. What does accreditation mean?

Questions about the U10–U12 centre

Can I book a place at the U10–U12 girls centre?

Not yet. The centre is not open for booking, and days and times are still to be confirmed. You can add your player to the waiting list using the enquiry form.

How do I join the U10–U12 waiting list?

Use the enquiry form, choose “Girls U10–U12 Waiting List” as the enquiry type, and include your player’s age and current club. We will be in touch when days, times and places are confirmed.

How much will the U10–U12 centre cost?

The planned price is £30 per month by direct debit. It applies to this group only and may differ from other sessions.

Is this the same as the U13–U15 girls centre?

No. It is a separate centre for younger girls. Our U13–U15 girls centre meets on Tuesdays, 6:00–7:00pm at Beckfoot School: see the Bingley centre page.
