Safe, qualified & accredited

1 Star England Football Accredited Club

England Football accreditation is awarded to clubs that meet its safeguarding and coaching standards. For youth football, that means DBS checked coaches and officials, a trained club welfare officer, and coaches who have completed FA coaching, first aid and safeguarding training.

All players are insured, and our FAQs explain what accreditation means for your child.

Who are the sessions for?

The current Bingley Development Centre is for U13–U15 girls who already play grassroots football and want additional player development.

Does this replace grassroots club training?

No. Sessions focus on individual development and are designed to complement normal club training and matches.

Where and when do you train?

The current group meets Tuesdays, 6:00–7:00pm at Beckfoot School in Bingley.

How can I ask about a place?
